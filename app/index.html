Roadmap to the Platinum

Built only from the official descriptions, in the order to play them. Five phases, one playthrough, no online trophies.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story

    Opening mission Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1–4 in order, finishing with the final mission. Take the character strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) as they appear. Trophies: Just Like Paradise · Paradise Lost · T**s Ass Drug Money · Vice City, Baby! · Top Of The World, Ma · You Gotta Know When to Get Off · Trust · Break the Bank · Family Matters · DWNFALL · Hi-Jacked · Snow Storm.

  2. Phase 2 — While you play: partner, combat, explore

    Do the partner trophies while your partner is with you, learn Combat Focus, and explore every corner of Leonida as you travel between missions. Trophies: Hot Together · Retail Therapy · Gunslinger · The Goodtime State · Whip It Good · Catch Me If You Can.

  3. Phase 3 — Strangers and side strands

    Clear stranger mission strands across the state, finish the character finals, and hunt Bret's unique animal. Trophies: Strangers & Freaks (8 strands) · Road to Rehabilitation · This Was No Boat Accident · The Lone Rider · Supply & Demand · Spin On This! · Fair Game.

  4. Phase 4 — Activities, races and the one missable trophy

    Stunts (250), Shooting Range golds, drone races, 20 race wins, fishing against Cal, one arcade game, bowling, and meditation at every spot. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot before Chapter 1 ends — it is the list's one missable trophy. Trophies: Cunning Stunts · The Shootist · Top Fun · Earn Cash, Not Kudos · Baited My Tackle · Stick Fiddler · Let’s Go Bowling · Positive Mental Attitude · Master of None · One Last Thing.

  5. Phase 5 — Collectibles, robberies and clean-up

    Card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, bank vaults, armored trucks, illegal operations for each gang, garages and Scooter Bros registrations — then check the in-game completion screen and close the gap to 100%. Trophies: Don’t Touch My Deck · You WAiNKer! · Sneakthief · Maximum Withdrawal · A Classic Blitz Play · Pest Control · Little Boxes · On the Spot · Dime on the Dollar · Treat Yo Self · A Long Road · Vice Got a Price!

⚠ Missable trophies — read before you play

1 trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida). Guides report that Lucia's balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter, and that the game has no mission replay. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

Every other trophy is earned by playing the story, exploring, or grinding a count — none of them can be locked out by story progress. Sources: Output Lag trophy list and KeenGamer roadmap, linked beside the trophy below.
